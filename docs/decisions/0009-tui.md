# 0009 TUI and the manager's overview

Status: accepted. First written 2026-10-10 (spike DIPO-6). Rewritten 2026-10-24 for the simplified design. The research behind the rewrite is in `docs/research/2026-10-24-simplification-audit.md`.

## Decision in short

The TUI is the first client. It is built with Ink 8 and React and is a thin client: it shows views the engine derived, and it acts only through commands (0005). For one office at a time it answers four questions: who works on what, how it is going, what needs the maintainer, and what it costs. The plain-text commands `dipo desk` and `dipo watch` read the same views.

## 1. A thin client

- **Only the contract.** The TUI imports only `contract` and `client` (0004). It reads `engine.hello`, subscribes to the software stream, reads `office.snapshot` for the chosen office and subscribes after its cursor (0005).
- **The store.** Its state is the `OfficeStore` from `client`. The store takes the snapshot, applies events by subject, drops duplicates by seq and handles a reset. It replaces views and never computes a column, health, phase or cost.
- **Keys from the engine.** Keys come from `commands.describe` and the actions the engine attaches to each desk item and park.
- **No model.** The overview, desk, digest and cost views are built by engine code from facts.
- **One store for every client.** A later web client uses the same store, so the two cannot disagree.

## 2. The vocabulary

One word per meaning, and every one is engine-derived.

- **Phase:** `setup` (claim and box), `build`, `review`, `test` (waiting for the maintainer's test), `merge`.
- **Health of a running session.** The engine derives it in this order; each state shows as a word plus a glyph:

  | Health | When |
  |---|---|
  | `lost` | The worker host died, or its exit is not processed yet (the story parks next) |
  | `looping` | The same failure signature twice in a row, or the last allowed loop |
  | `stuck` | No output for `timeouts.workerStallMinutes` (default 20) |
  | `quiet` | No output for `timeouts.workerQuietMinutes` (default 3) |
  | `busy` | Output within the quiet window |

  The watchdog facts behind it (`no-output`, `same-failure`, `host-lost`) reach the client as the evidence of a `work.observed` event. They are not a second vocabulary.
- **Lifecycle** is 0012's.
- **Queue column:** `draft`, `refined`, `ready`, `waiting` (Ready, but a dependency is not Done), `running`, `review`, `desk`, `done`.
- **Evidence labels.** Each value the engine sends carries one: `checked` (the engine measured it), `claimed` (the worker or reviewer said so), `estimated` (computed by a rule) or `none` (no fact, never guessed). The TUI shows no evidence markers.
- **Your desk** is everything that waits on the maintainer (0020).

## 3. Views

Rooms are numbered `1` to `5`: Desk, Floor, Queue, Retro and Plan. `O` opens Offices, and `Enter` drills in.

- **Offices** (`O`). One row per office, NEEDS YOU first: running, needs you, Ready, today's spend, last activity as idle N days, and the live, starts-paused and spend-alert marks. `a` opens a repository path.
- **Floor** (`2`, home). The sections RUNNING, with the run slots in use, and IN REVIEW, one card per story, then UP NEXT. A story is in one of them, never both: a story in review with no session, or whose session is the reviewer, is IN REVIEW. A card shows the story line, the pipeline with its loop or round, a `now:` line with the newest worker line and the budget bar, and criteria, review round and model. Health shows only when it is `stuck`, `looping` or `lost`. With nothing running, one line says why. UP NEXT is one sentence from the chain, such as "When DIPO-447 merges → 448, 449 start in parallel". Stories that wait on the maintainer are on the Desk, Ready and waiting ones in the Queue.
- **The pipeline.** One component draws `setup ━━ build ━━ review ━━ test ━━ merge`, with verify and CI as marks inside a phase, never phases of their own. Every view that shows a phase uses it.
- **Queue** (`3`). The groups READY, WAITING ON A DEPENDENCY and REFINED, and `.` shows the Done stories. Each row has its tier and a reason: next up, `needs N✗` (the dependency is parked, the maintainer's to clear), `needs N→` (it moves by itself), a bare `needs N` (it waits too), or the first Ready gate failure with the rule id. From 100 columns a preview of the selected story sits at the right: its gate with the rule count, what it needs, what it unblocks, the files it touches and the chain picture, which points to the Desk when a park blocks the chain.
- **The chain and the gate.** The Floor and the Queue read `office.chain`: the dependency graph of the waiting stories as levels, with a state per story (`parked`, `moving` for a Ready, running or in-review story, or `waiting`) and the stories each one unblocks. The Queue reads `story.gate` for the selected story: passed or not, the rule count, the failures and the files it touches. The TUI only joins what the engine sent; it derives no state, level or count.
- **Story detail** (`Enter`). Facts on the left. On the right, the worker output from `work.output` and `work.outputRange`, or the stored prompt (`i`). An ended run is shown read-only.
- **Desk** (`1`). The `office.desk` read, in its order. From 100 columns it is a list beside a preview of the focused item. The list has one header per kind, in the order the items come in; the TUI never sorts them. A row shows the kind's glyph, the title and the age, with the reason and `blocks N` below it. The preview shows everything the item needs the maintainer to decide, wrapped and never cut: the detail, what it blocks, since when, the hand step, the item's actions as buttons bound to keys, the line saying what each action does, and its `dipo` command line. Below 100 columns the Desk is the list alone, and `Enter` opens the preview full screen.
- **Since you left.** Above the wide Desk's list, the Since band says what changed since the TUI last saw the office: stories merged, new proposals and the estimated spend, from `office.digest`. It shows only when one of them is above zero, and any key dismisses it. The TUI keeps in memory the time it last switched away from each office and passes it; an office not shown yet in this run counts from when it opens.
- **Planning** (`5`). The office's planning session (0022): with no open session it asks for a kind and a goal and starts one; with one it shows kind, goal, state, live spend and the streamed answers, is one conversation column with one input at the foot (`Enter` sends; `Shift+Enter`, `Option+Enter` or a backslash before `Enter` adds a line; `Tab` opens the stories fold-out), shows Claude's question blocks as a dialog, and shows each proposal that still needs a decision inline as a compact card acted on with `c` confirm, `e` edit and `x` decline (`Up` on an empty input moves focus to the cards, `Esc` or any other printable key back to the input). A confirmed proposal leaves the cards at once and stays as one line, `filing…` and then `filed as DIPO-n`; a declined one leaves. `/end` opens the Ready step: the session's filed and edited stories not yet Ready, all ticked, `space` ticks one, `Y` sets the ticked ones Ready and ends the session, `n` ends with none Ready and `Esc` keeps the session open.
- **Retro** (`4`). The retro numbers dipo computes by code (0022): parks by reason, rounds and loops, cost and time per tier, the rework share, where stories came from and what the audit turned away; `d` discusses them in a plan.
- **Test checklist** (`t` on a desk item; `y` passes, `n` fails). `test.start` returns the test box's URL and the manual steps. Ticks are local UI state, and the outcome is one `test.record`.

A cost view by story, role and tier is Later.

## 4. Actions

Every action is one command (0005) with `origin: "tui"` and a fresh request id. The TUI asks for confirmation where `commands.describe` says `confirm`, and for the typed story id where it says `irreversible` (`work.discard`, and `story.drop` with `--delete-branch`).

| Key | Command |
|---|---|
| `a` | `work.answer` |
| `s` | `work.steer` |
| `r` | `work.resume` |
| `p` | `story.park` (asks for a note) |
| `D` | `work.discard` (typed) |
| `X` | `story.drop` |
| `y` / `n` | `gate.approve` / `gate.reject` (a reject needs a note); on a proposed story, `idea.accept` / `idea.dismiss`; `n` on a decision note, `note.dismiss` |
| `i` on the desk | `idea.add`: the maintainer's quick idea, to the audit (0022) |
| `t`, then pass or fail | `test.start`, then `test.record` (a fail needs a note) |
| `r` in the Queue | `work.run` |
| `c` | copy the id |

- **Errors** show in the footer. A retryable error offers a retry with the same request id. `changed: false` shows "already so".
- **Read-your-writes.** After a reply the TUI waits until its stream passes the reply's cursor before it marks the action done.

**Global keys:** `1` to `5`, `O`, `Tab`, `j` and `k` or the arrows, `Enter`, `Esc`, `/` to filter, `:` for the command palette, which also lists the settings, `?` for help, and `q` twice within 2 s to quit. Uppercase keys end or replace work; `r` runs a Ready story from the Queue. No action fires without its target visible on screen.

## 5. Connection and daemon states

- **Start.** `dipo tui` starts the daemon if needed and opens the office of the current directory or the last one used.
- **Offline.** The header shows `OFFLINE since <time>` and values keep their age. Action keys are disabled and typed text is kept. The client reconnects with backoff (0.5 s, doubling to 5 s) and subscribes after its last cursor.
- **A reset.** On `stream.resetRequired` the store reloads and says so once.
- **The header.** dipo / office, `+N elsewhere · O`, the daemon dot and word, spend today and the time. Recovering, draining and starts paused show as header chips, and daemon stopped and a database problem get a full body with `r` restart, `l` log and `c` copy.

## 6. Spend alerts

Spend alerts count all offices together, per UTC day and UTC month, against the limits in `<state>/config.yaml`. They count tokens as the budget counts them (0008). The cost the CLI reports is shown next to them, never alerted on. An alert shows on Offices, the `dipo watch` header and the `dipo desk` count line, and once as an `engine.notice` per window and level.

## 7. Terminals and accessibility

- **Width.** At 120 columns everything shows. From 80 to 119 the wide columns drop. From 60 to 79 each running story is a two-line card. Below 60 columns or 16 rows, one short list shows. There is never horizontal scrolling.
- **Screen.** The alternate screen is the default, and `--inline` renders in the scrollback. `dipo desk` and `dipo watch` are plain text and never use Ink.
- **No colour-only signals.** Every state is a word plus an ASCII glyph. `NO_COLOR`, `--no-color` and `--ascii` are honoured. Tests assert on frames with ANSI stripped.
- **Performance.** The store batches events at most 10 times a second. Output panes keep only the visible lines and fetch the rest.

## 8. Desk notifications

When an item is added to an office's desk, the daemon posts one macOS notification naming the office, the story and what waits. An item already shown is not shown again. `notify.desk` in `<state>/config.yaml` turns this off, and it is on by default. The post goes through the platform module, and a failed post is logged and never stops the daemon. A declared "online" presence for live questions is Later (0003).
