--- notes ---
`plan` groups every story that is not Done by milestone, in the backlog's milestone order: the milestone head (bold hi name, a bar of Done over all in ok and faint, `done/total` dim, two trailing spaces), then its open stories in chain order with 05-queue.txt's row (glyph, dim id, title padded to 30, dim tier, reason). A milestone with everything Done folds to one ok line. At most four stories show per milestone; `+ N more · ⏎ unfolds` folds the rest. Stories with no milestone come last under NO MILESTONE.
⏎ on a milestone head folds and unfolds it; ⏎ on a story opens it (04-story-reader.txt). The preview of a milestone: its description from the backlog (bold hi, wrapped), Progress, Ready, Not yet (why the rest is not Ready, in counts), Next (the first story to act on), the milestone's CHAIN, and a dim line pointing at what blocks it. The preview of a story is 05-queue.txt's.
space and Y work as in 05-queue.txt on Refined rows. Everything here comes from the engine (`office.milestones`, a new read): the TUI groups and counts nothing itself.
