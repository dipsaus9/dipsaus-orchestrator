The room keeps the keyboard: every lowercase key is the room's verb, as in TUI v2.
While the room has the keyboard the panel is quiet: the conversation in its roles, the input dim (` > ` dim and `` ` to type``). `` ` `` moves the keyboard to the chat: the input turns acc with a cursor (05-run-with-dock-wide.txt), the footer shows the chat's keys, `esc` gives the keyboard back.
`ask about this` lists questions code builds from the focused item's kind (MERGE: test, unblock, advisory). No model call happens until you send one; ↑ from the input or a click picks it.
The panel follows the room's focus: the context line changes when you move; the conversation does not.
