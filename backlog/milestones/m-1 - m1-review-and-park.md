## Description

M1a, the unattended review-and-park loop: the engine opens the story PR; a reviewer session reviews it against the story; CI failures, review findings and merge conflicts go back to the story's worker by code, with evidence, deduplicated by send-once signatures keyed on commit, within caps (research ideas 5, 14 and 17); an approved PR merges through the merge slot; every park reason can be resumed; hold, drop, discard, steer and pause; a plain-text `dipo desk`. M1b: the morning triage view in the TUI, the test-story command with pass or fail feedback, the scan's AI fallback and confirm command, daily and monthly spend alerts (idea 6), and skills in worker sessions. See the 0003 amendment of 2026-10-16.
